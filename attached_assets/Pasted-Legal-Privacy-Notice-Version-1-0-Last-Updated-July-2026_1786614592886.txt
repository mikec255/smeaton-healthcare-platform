SY
The Systems We Use
To deliver our services safely and efficiently, Smeaton Healthcare uses secure digital systems.

CareLogr

Our secure care management and recruitment platform, used to manage customer enquiries, recruitment, care plans, risk assessments, medication records, visit scheduling, incident reporting, staff training and compliance.

Website Hosting (Microsoft Azure)

Our website is securely hosted using Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure. Information submitted through our website is encrypted during transmission.

Microsoft 365

Used to manage business email, documents, collaboration and internal communications.

Payroll Systems

We use secure payroll software to process salaries, pensions, statutory payments and employment records.

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

We may use AI within CareLogr to support administrative functions including quality assurance, audit support, compliance monitoring and reporting. Decisions that could significantly affect individuals are never made solely by AI and are always subject to human review.

All third-party providers are required to process personal information securely and in accordance with UK data protection legislation.
